4. LUU Y KHI PHAN TICH

- Attrition co mat can bang lop: khoang 16,1% Yes va 83,9% No. Khi xay dung
  mo hinh, can xem xet cac chi so phu hop, dac biet la recall, precision,
  F1-score va AUC-ROC; khong chi dua vao accuracy.
- Education, EnvironmentSatisfaction, JobInvolvement, JobLevel,
  JobSatisfaction, PerformanceRating, RelationshipSatisfaction,
  StockOptionLevel va WorkLifeBalance la ma thu tu/muc do. Can xem xet y nghia
  cua ma truoc khi chuan hoa hoac coi chung nhu bien lien tuc.
- EmployeeNumber la dinh danh, khong nen dua truc tiep vao mo hinh nhu mot
  bien lien tuc.
- EmployeeCount, Over18 va StandardHours la cot hang trong bo du lieu, thuong
  khong mang thong tin phan biet de mo hinh.
- MonthlyIncome va cac bien so nam co the co gia tri ngoai lai theo quy tac
  IQR; can phan biet ngoai lai thong ke voi gia tri sai du lieu.
- Don vi cua DailyRate, HourlyRate, MonthlyIncome, MonthlyRate va
  DistanceFromHome khong duoc xac dinh trong tu dien du lieu.
- JobRole lien quan den Department; Manager co the xuat hien trong ca ba
  phong ban.
